1

Sign it offline

In your own wallet. Bitcoin: Sparrow or Electrum sign with no internet. Solana: use a durable nonce so it never expires.

2

LASTSYNC carries it

Paste it or scan its QR code. It shows exactly who gets paid and how much, checks the signature, and shares it with your group.

3

First signal sends it

Laptop or any phone in the group — whoever gets a connection first broadcasts it, and LASTSYNC tracks it to confirmation.

The network goes down

Power cut, storm, outage. Phones lose signal. The internet is gone.

3

Everything still runs

LASTSYNC runs on your PC or USB stick. Your household stays in sync over local Wi-Fi.
