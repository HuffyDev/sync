01

Search & Ask

Every guide, PDF and note on your machine. Answers quote the source, and say so when they don't know.

02

Offline street maps

Real streets, buildings and emergency places. Save your town once; use it with zero signal. Range rings, routes, GPS.

03

On-device AI

Three model sizes that run in your browser on your own graphics chip. No servers, no account.

04

Encyclopedia

64 survival-relevant Wikipedia articles — medicine, water, power, navigation, disasters — saved for offline.

05

Household check-ins

Your laptop hosts the group over local Wi-Fi. Phones scan a QR code and send I'm safe, Meet here or I need help.

06

Plan & gear

Contacts, rally points and a grab list, a printable one-page backup, and a gear checklist with an essentials score.

1

Set up while you're online

Fill in your plan, save your area's map, download the encyclopedia and an AI model.

2

The network goes down

Power cut, storm, outage. Phones lose signal. The internet is gone.

3

Everything still runs

LASTSYNC runs on your PC or USB stick. Your household stays in sync over local Wi-Fi.
